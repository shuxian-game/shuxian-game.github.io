What is 股海修仙傳

股海修仙傳 transforms stock research and portfolio organization into a cultivation-inspired game experience.

Players discover companies, build Fish Cards, organize holdings, compare groups of companies, and learn to understand investment information through an interactive world.

將股票研究、持股管理與投資學習轉化為修仙世界中的尋魚、養魚、魚卡與宗門修行。

Currently in Development

The project is being independently developed for Windows and is planned for distribution through Steam.
